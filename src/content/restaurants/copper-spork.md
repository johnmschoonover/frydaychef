Copper Spork runs three seatings per night, each one feeling like a communal dinner party. The menu swings seasonal, but always leans on smoke and mountain herbs. Come hungry, linger between courses on the back porch, and let the hosts stack your table with unexpected palate cleansers (last visit: frozen muscadine grapes dusted in citric acid).
