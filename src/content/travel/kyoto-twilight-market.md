Kyoto’s evening markets feel like a soft exhale after a packed sightseeing day. Pontocho Alley glows with paper lanterns while narrow balconies hover above the Kamogawa. Taprooms pour tiny cups of local sake, and street vendors baste yakitori with layer after layer of tare until it practically lacquer-coats the skewers.

Make time for Nishiki Market just before closing. The bustle slows, vendors chat freely, and you can sample everything from amazake to freshly torched mackerel. Take your bounty to the river steps, listen to buskers setting up for the night, and let the city wind down around you.
