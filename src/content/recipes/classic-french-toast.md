---
title: Classic French Toast
date: "2024-04-05"
tags:
  - breakfast
  - quick
servings: 4
prep: 10m
cook: 15m
total: 25m
ingredients:
  - 8 slices brioche, slightly stale
  - 4 large eggs
  - 1 cup whole milk
  - 2 tbsp maple syrup
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  - Butter for the pan
  - Fresh berries and powdered sugar for serving
steps:
  - Whisk eggs, milk, syrup, vanilla, and cinnamon until smooth.
  - Dip brioche slices for 10 seconds a side, letting excess drip off.
  - Cook on a buttered skillet over medium heat until deeply golden, about 3 minutes per side.
  - Serve warm with berries and powdered sugar.

A weekend-ready brunch staple with crisp edges, custardy centers, and just enough spice.
