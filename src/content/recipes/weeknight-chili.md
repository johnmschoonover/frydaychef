---
title: Weeknight Chili
date: "2024-03-22"
tags:
  - dinner
  - comfort
servings: 6
prep: 15m
cook: 35m
total: 50m
ingredients:
  - 1 lb ground beef or turkey
  - 1 small onion, diced
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  - 2 tbsp chili powder
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - 1 tsp ground cumin
  - 1 28-oz can crushed tomatoes
  - 1 15-oz can kidney beans, rinsed
  - 1 cup beef or veggie stock
  - Salt, pepper, lime wedges
steps:
  - Brown meat with onion, garlic, and pepper in a heavy pot until the veggies soften.
  - Stir in spices and toast for 1 minute to bloom.
  - Add tomatoes, beans, and stock; simmer uncovered 20–25 minutes until thick.
  - Season to taste and serve with lime, scallions, and crunchy toppings.

A smoky, spoon-coating chili that still fits between work and movie night.
