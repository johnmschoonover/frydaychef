title: Bistro Grilled Cheese

tags:

servings: 2
prep: 10m
cook: 12m
total: 22m
ingredients:
  - 4 slices sourdough bread
  - 2 tbsp butter
  - 1 small shallot, thinly sliced
  - 1 cup mushrooms, thinly sliced
  - 1 tsp thyme leaves
  - 4 slices gruyère
  - 2 tbsp fig jam
  - Arugula for serving
steps:
  - Sauté shallot, mushrooms, and thyme in 1 tbsp butter until browned; set aside.
  - Butter bread, spread fig jam inside, layer cheese, mushroom mixture, and a handful of arugula.
  - Grill over medium-low heat until the cheese melts and the bread crisps, about 4 minutes per side.
