If your ideal movie snack is that neon-gold, shatter-crisp theater popcorn, this
is the stovetop version dialed in for repeatable results. You’ll start with
trustworthy kernels, real Flavacol, and butter-flavored coconut oil, then lean
on heat control and vented foil to keep every batch light, dry, and crunchy
instead of greasy or soggy.

The method is built for feel and safety: pre-season the kernels, fully preheat
the oil with a single test pop, shake the pot with oven mitts on, and treat the
foil lid like a cloud of steam as you lift it away. A big finishing bowl
(10-quart if you have it) plus a double Flavacol toss and optional butter
drizzle give you that true theater flavor without guessing at the sequence.
