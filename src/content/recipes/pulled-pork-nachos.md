phases:
  - title: Warm the pork and sauce
    ingredients:
      - Smoked pulled pork
      - Bold, sweet BBQ sauce
    steps:
      - text:
          Add the pulled pork and BBQ sauce to a zip-top or vacuum bag and warm
          in a 165°F sous vide bath until hot and glossy.
        detail:
          Warming now keeps the chips from steaming later—bring the pork to
          serving temp before layering.
        completeLabel: Pork warmed and sauced
  - title: Make the sodium citrate cheese sauce
    ingredients:
      - 115 g smoked sharp white cheddar, freshly grated
      - 115 g water
      - 4.6 g sodium citrate
      - 1-2 tsp pickled jalapeño brine (just a splash)
    steps:
      - text:
          Heat the water in a small saucepan over medium and whisk in the sodium
          citrate until dissolved.
        completeLabel: Sodium citrate dissolved
      - text:
          Add a small splash of jalapeño brine and stir to combine. Start with a
          teaspoon; you can add another if you want a little more tang and heat.
        completeLabel: Brine stirred in
      - text:
          Add the grated cheese in small handfuls, stirring constantly until the
          sauce is smooth, glossy, and silky.
        detail:
          Keep the heat at medium to avoid scorching. The sauce will stay
          pourable even as it cools.
        completeLabel: Cheese sauce silky
  - title: Build and bake the nachos
    ingredients:
      - Thick tortilla chips
      - 1 cup shredded cheese (Monterey Jack + sharp cheddar)
      - Cheese sauce from above
      - Warmed pulled pork
      - Pickled jalapeños
      - Pickled red onions (optional)
      - Black or pinto beans (optional)
      - Sliced green onions
      - Sour cream or crema
      - Extra BBQ sauce for drizzling
    steps:
      - text:
          Preheat the oven to 400°F and line a sturdy sheet pan with parchment.
        completeLabel: Pan prepped
      - text:
          Build a single deliberate layer in this order—chips, shredded cheese,
          warm pulled pork, more shredded cheese, jalapeños, and a light drizzle
          of BBQ sauce.
        detail:
          Avoid stacking multiple layers—this keeps the chips crisp instead of
          casserole-soft.
        completeLabel: First layer built
      - text:
          Spoon light streaks of cheese sauce across the chips without flooding
          them. Keep it to stripes rather than a full coat.
        completeLabel: Cheese layered
      - text:
          Bake at 350°F until the cheese is fully melted, then move to the
          broiler to brown the edges and cheese bits.
        warningLabel: Watch closely under the broiler so the chips don’t scorch.
        completeLabel: Baked and broiled
      - text:
          Finish with sour cream or crema, green onions, optional beans or
          pickled onions, and a final drizzle of BBQ sauce if it looks like it
          wants it.
        completeLabel: Finished and ready to serve

Built for a sheet pan and a squeeze bottle, these pulled pork nachos marry
barbecue sweetness with Tex-Mex crunch. Warmed pork keeps the chips crisp,
sodium citrate cheese sauce stays silky, and a single intentional layer prevents
the dreaded soggy middle.

Finish them under the broiler, scatter bright toppings, and drizzle just enough
extra sauce for shine. They’re best when the pork is already cooked—freeze
portions from your last shoulder so nacho night is always within reach.
